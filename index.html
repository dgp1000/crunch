    How Crunch works

    • Tap a tile, an operator, then another tile. They combine into a new tile.
    • Make the target and you're done. Faster means more points; you've got 5 minutes.
    • Each tile can be used once. No negatives, no fractions.

    e.g. target 472 → 75 × 6 = 450, then 450 + 22 = 472
